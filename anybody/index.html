Your land is just a land. Your company is just another company. In short your asset alone is fragile. One market difference, one bad season, one missed trend and they stop working.

In AnyBody nothing works alone.

Your asset doesn't have to be perfect. It doesn't have to be complete . It just has to exist.

Because in the AnyBody Pool, what your company lack the other company have surplus of it, and because of that nothing lacks anything. Your weakness is covered by the others' strength. Your asset value no longer depends on what it can do but what everything together can do.

That means your returns don't come from one asset's performance, but from a combined system, that is structure to work in all case.

Your job is to own, our job is to make you benefit more than you will alone.

It's the first structure where land, companies, intellectual property, other assets are pooled into one system, not to be managed separately but to function as a single diversified system.

It's the first box in the diagram, in this box three things go:

  • Land (from Landowners)
  • Companies (from Companies Owners)
  • Other Assets (from Other assets owners)

Project funding comes directly from the collective partnership agreement that give AnyBody development rights, signed by all asset owners. We use this single, unified contract, not the individual land, companies or IP, as collateral to secure finance for projects.

Most importantly we use the synergy and connections in between all of the assets and make them work together to bring the best out of each other.

Projects are not predetermined.

After the pool is complete and finance is secured, we analyze the pool: all land, companies, and assets, their locations, capabilities and market conditions. We then calculate what's going to be most profitable development and strategic uses of the budget and leverages the global spread of the pool.

We don't decide in advance because the best projects for the pool depends on what is in the pool. The pool decides through data, locations, and market demand.

55% of all profits generated across all projects combined will be distributed annually, forever, to all asset owners in the pool. This will be proportional to the initial value of their assets when it was contributed to the pool.

You are paid from the success of the entire system, not just your individual asset. the strength of the pool ensures this share is stable and growing, because your return is backed by dozens of assets, not one.
